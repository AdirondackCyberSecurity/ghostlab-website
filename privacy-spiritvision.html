GhostLab: Spirit Vision Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “GhostLab: Spirit Vision”

Spirit Vision is an experimental, AI-powered investigative tool. To generate an image during a session, it sends a text summary of the moment to GhostLab’s server and two outside AI services. This page says exactly what that summary holds, who receives it, and what stays on your phone. The app asks your permission before your first session, and you can withdraw it in Settings.

Summary

  • Your voice stays on your iPhone. Sounds are recognised on the device, and no audio leaves the phone.
  • Your recordings and snapshots stay on your iPhone unless you share them.
  • During a session, text goes to AI services. Sensor readings and the names of sounds go to Anthropic, and an image description goes to Runware.
  • GhostLab does not store what is sent. It passes through our server and is never written to its storage.
  • The camera is never used, and your location stays on your phone. During a session the app uses it to tell whether you seem to be in a car, indoors or outdoors, and only that one word is sent.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell personal data.
  • No sign-in. The app uses a random account number that names nobody.

What is sent during a session

Nothing is sent until you agree, and nothing is sent outside a session. During a session, each time a sensor change calls for a vision, the app sends:

  • the names of sounds the phone recognised, such as a knock or footsteps
  • the sensor change that set it off, such as a shift in the magnetic field, motion or air pressure
  • whether the phone seems to be in a car, indoors or outdoors, worked out on the phone
  • the place you chose in the app, your local time and your time zone offset
  • a short written memory of the session so far, which the phone holds and sends back with each moment

Who receives it

  • GhostLab’s server, which runs on Cloudflare, receives everything above and passes it on. It keeps none of it. Short-lived service logs record that a vision happened, what it cost, and a one-word label for it. Cloudflare handles your IP address to carry the connection, and we do not store it.
  • Anthropic receives everything above, so that its Claude model can plan each image. Anthropic says it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, and may keep them longer where its usage policy or the law requires. Anthropic’s retention page has the details.
  • Runware receives the image description Claude writes, and generates the image from it. Runware says it does not use API data to train models and does not keep generated images long term. Runware’s data page has the details.

If you’d rather not share this, press Not now when the app asks, or choose Stop sharing in Settings. Sessions need sharing to bring visions, and everything else in the app keeps working.

What GhostLab keeps

To enforce each plan’s daily limits, and to keep pack balances safe across a reinstall, our server keeps a small record for each account:

  • a random account number, made by the app and kept in your iCloud Keychain
  • a public key from Apple’s App Attest, which proves requests come from a real device running this app
  • today’s count of visions used and what they cost us
  • your plan, its renewal date and Apple’s transaction numbers for your purchases
  • your vision pack balance

This record holds no name, email address or pictures. It stays while your account is in use. To have it deleted, email us at the address below with your App Store order number if you bought something.

The microphone, sensors and location

  • During a session the app measures how loud the room is and recognises sounds, on the device.
  • When you record a session, the room’s sound is saved in that recording on your phone.
  • Motion, magnetic and pressure readings drive the smoke and the readings on screen.
  • With your permission, your location, your motion and whether you are on Wi-Fi tell the app if the phone seems to be in a car, indoors or outdoors. Location is read only during a session, is never stored and never leaves the phone.
  • If you turn off a permission in iOS Settings, the app shows that reading as off.

Data the app keeps on your device

  • Snapshots and recordings are saved in the app’s Library. Like other app documents, they are included in your device backup. Deleting an item, or the app, removes them.
  • Settings, such as your chosen place and whether you agreed to sharing, stay on the device.
  • Your account number stays in your iCloud Keychain, so it can outlast deleting the app.

Sharing and Photos

  • Saving to Photos uses add-only access. The app cannot see anything already in your photo library.
  • The share sheet sends a capture wherever you choose. Once it leaves the app, whoever receives it decides what happens to it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and vision packs are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app passes Apple’s signed record of each purchase to our server, which reads the product, dates and transaction numbers from it. We receive no name, email address or payment details. Apple tells our server about refunds so that it can adjust your plan or pack balance.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not upload your audio, recordings or snapshots.
  • We do not use the camera, and your location never leaves your phone.

Children

The app carries the age rating shown on its App Store page and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the record for that account.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If the list of AI services changes, the app will ask your permission again.
