GhostLab Signal Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “GhostLab Signal”

GhostLab Signal is a spirit box instrument for entertainment. It plays prerecorded and generated voices at random and records each session on your phone. It has no user accounts, no servers of our own, and it collects no data about you.

Summary

  • No data is collected. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The microphone is never used. The app does not ask for microphone access and never listens.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.

Data the app keeps on your device

  • Session recordings — when you stop a session, the audio the app played is saved in the app’s own storage on your phone, with its date and length. Recordings are never uploaded. Deleting a recording in the Library, or deleting the app, removes them.
  • Settings — your echo, reverb, white noise, and sweep rate choices are kept on the device so the app opens the way you left it.

Purchases

GhostLab Signal offers a free 48-hour trial and a one-time lifetime unlock. Both are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you. You can restore the unlock on a new device from inside the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers.
  • We do not record your voice or the room around you.

Children

The app carries the age rating shown on its App Store page. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app collects none from anyone.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
