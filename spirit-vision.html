Spirit Vision

AI visions from your sensors.

GhostLab: Spirit Vision is an experimental, AI-powered investigative tool for your paranormal kit. It uses AI to generate images in the smoke from changes in your phone's motion, magnetic, pressure and sound readings, such as a knock or footsteps. What each image means to you is yours to decide.

Free to start: black smoke and 2 visions a day, with 5 on your first day. Plans start at $1.99 a month.

The session

Start a session and set your phone down.

The plate shows the magnetic field, motion, air pressure and sound as they change. A sharp change or a knock can bring a vision, and the image rises out of the smoke as a picture alone.

  • Four live readings from your phone's own sensors
  • Images grow more intense as the sensors fire more often
  • It remembers what it showed earlier in a session
  • Ordinary things in the room move these readings too
Places

Black smoke and five places.

Everyone starts in black smoke. Spirit Vision Pro opens the hallway, the graveyard, the abandoned warehouse, the abandoned house and the fiery landscape, each with its own smoke.

In black smoke, your location and motion show whether you're in a car, indoors or outside, so each vision suits where you are. A place you choose stays as you chose it.

Run a session

Four steps into the smoke.

A picture takes about six seconds.

  1. 01 Press Start session

    The first time, the app shows what goes to its AI services and asks your permission.

  2. 02 Set your phone down

    Pick a spot in the room. The plate shows the readings as they change.

  3. 03 Watch the smoke

    A knock, footsteps or a sharp change in the readings can bring a vision.

  4. 04 Keep what you see

    Snapshots carry the time and the readings. Record a whole session with the sound of the room. Both wait in the Library, ready to save or share.

iPhone and iPad

Runs on iOS 17 or later and needs an internet connection to generate images. No sign-in. Rated 12+ on the App Store.

Your privacy

With your permission, your sensor readings, the names of sounds the phone heard and whether you seem to be in a car, indoors or outside go to GhostLab's server and Anthropic, and an image description goes to Runware. No audio is sent, your location stays on your phone, and there are no ads and no tracking. Privacy policy

Before you start

Visions can be frightening, and some show the dead. The paranormal is still unproven, so please don't lean on a vision for big decisions or when you're grieving.

Take it on your next investigation.

GhostLab: Spirit Vision is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. It's free to start, with plans from $1.99 a month, and it runs on its own next to GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit or any other kit you carry.
