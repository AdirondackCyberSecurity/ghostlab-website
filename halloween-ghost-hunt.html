Halloween 2026

Your Halloween Ghost Hunt step by step.

A simple plan for Halloween night with the three GhostLab apps. Map the room with Spirit Detector, log EMF readings and record EVP audio with Paranormal Toolkit, then run a spirit box session with Dead Channel. Bring a friend, get permission, and have fun with it.

Jump to the checklist

For fun and entertainment. The apps log readings, record audio, map surfaces and play sound. They do not detect or prove anything.

Before you go

Get ready in daylight.

Sort these out before it gets dark.

Get permission

Only hunt where you are allowed to be. Ask the owner or manager first. Many parks and cemeteries close at dusk, so check the posted hours. If anyone asks you to leave, leave.

Stay safe

Wear sturdy shoes and a warm layer, carry a flashlight, and watch your step on stairs and uneven ground. Skip abandoned or damaged buildings. If something feels unsafe, call it a night.

Bring a friend

Never go alone. Tell someone who is staying home where you will be and when you expect to be back. Agree on a time to check in.

Charge your phone

Start with a full battery and bring a power bank and cable. Scanning, the flashlight and long recordings all use battery quickly.

Pack headphones

Headphones make quiet recordings much easier to hear on playback. Keep one ear free while you walk around so you can still hear the room.

Take notes

Bring a notebook and pen, or use a notes app. Write down the time, the room and anything you notice, like a draft, a creaky floor or a car going by.

The plan

Three steps for Halloween night.

Each app works on its own. Use all three in this order, or skip any step you do not have the app or device for.

Step 1

Map the room with Spirit Detector

Needs a LiDAR scanner. Spirit Detector runs on iPhone 12 Pro or a later Pro model, or an iPad Pro from 2020 on, with iOS 17 or later. It is not available on Android. If nobody in your group has one, go straight to Step 2.

Spirit Detector uses the LiDAR scanner to draw the room as dots, one measured distance at a time. Do this first, before you turn the lights down, so you have a record of the doorways, stairs and furniture to plan around and check against later.

  • Hold the phone at chest height and let the room fill in for a few seconds before you move
  • Turn slowly. Fast turns smear the map
  • Tap Freeze to stop adding dots, then walk around what you have
  • Tap Record for a short video of each room. It saves to the Library
  • Glass, mirrors and dark paint can confuse the scanner, and people walking through leave trails. That is normal for LiDAR

Step 2

Run EMF and EVP sessions in Paranormal Toolkit

Open GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit and start with the EMF tile. It reads the phone's magnetometer and shows the level on green to red LED bars. Then switch to Classic EVP, which records clean microphone audio for you to play back later.

  • Take an EMF reading in each room before you start, so you know what normal looks like there
  • Outlets, wiring, appliances, speakers and other phones all move the meter. Note where they are
  • Walk slowly and write down the time and place whenever the reading changes
  • For EVP, ask everyone to stay still and quiet. Say the time and your name at the start of each recording
  • Ask a short question, then wait 20 to 30 seconds in silence before the next one
  • Call out your own noises, like "that was me" or "car outside", so playback is easier
  • Keep each recording to about 5 minutes. Short clips are easier to review

Step 3

Do a Dead Channel spirit box session

GhostLab: Dead Channel is a spirit box app. It sweeps through eight banks of recorded voice clips, breaths and static, a few at a time with silence in between, chosen at random. The app never uses the microphone, so it does not hear your questions. Everyone listens and decides for themselves what they heard.

  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons and tap Start
  • Ask one short question out loud, then stay quiet for about 30 seconds
  • The phone taps and the screen flashes red when it plays a longer clip. Read the session clock out loud when that happens
  • Everyone writes down what they heard before anyone says it out loud. Then compare notes
  • Run a second phone or voice recorder if you want your questions on tape. Dead Channel saves only its own audio
  • Tap Stop. The session saves to the Library so you can play it back later

Timing

A simple 90 minute night.

Halloween is on a Saturday this year. Visit the location in daylight first, then start after dark.

  1. 0:00Walk the location with the lights on. Agree on which rooms you will use.
  2. 0:10Step 1. Map each room with Spirit Detector and record a short video.
  3. 0:25Step 2. EMF readings in each room, then EVP recordings of about 5 minutes each.
  4. 0:55Step 3. A Dead Channel session of 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. 1:15Pack up, count heads, and leave the place the way you found it.
  6. LaterPlay back the recordings with headphones and line up your notes by time.
After the hunt

Review it together.

Play back your EVP clips and the Dead Channel session with headphones, and go through everyone's notes side by side. FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need all three apps?

No. Each app works on its own. Paranormal Toolkit covers EMF and EVP for free on iPhone, iPad and Android. Add Spirit Detector if you have a LiDAR iPhone or iPad Pro, and Dead Channel if you want a separate spirit box app.

Which devices can run Spirit Detector?

iPhone 12 Pro or a later Pro model, or an iPad Pro from 2020 on, running iOS 17 or later. It needs the LiDAR scanner, so it does not run on other iPhones and is not available on Android.

When should I start the free trials?

The Spirit Detector trial starts with your first scan, and the Dead Channel trial starts when you begin it. Each lasts 48 hours. If you want to use them on Halloween night without buying, start them no more than two days before.

Can the apps detect ghosts?

No. The apps log sensor readings, record audio, map surfaces and play sound. They do not detect, measure or prove anything paranormal. GhostLab is for entertainment.

Where is a good place to hunt?
