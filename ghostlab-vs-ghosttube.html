Deep dives

The differences that matter in the field.

Pricing

GhostLab Pro is $9.99 one-time. You get 48 hours of the full Pro suite, then you buy once. No subscription. GhostTube premium features typically use subscriptions, per app or as a bundle. Historical App Store examples have been about $2.99-$3.99 a month or about $11.99-$14.99 a year per app, with a Bundle around $49.99. Check the live store before you budget; those figures are not guaranteed.

Privacy

GhostLab does not ask for an account. There are no ads and no analytics SDKs. Sessions stay on the device until you export them. GhostTube’s community and online features, and some tools, may need an account or an in-app purchase. If you want everything local, GhostLab is built that way.

Field workflow

GhostLab is built around one loop: pick a tile, run the tools, tag hits, then review. Same workflow whether you are solo or on Team radio. You do not bounce between apps to change from EMF to EVP to SLS. GhostTube’s strength is the opposite shape: a suite you assemble from dedicated apps.

Spirit Speak vs dictionary-style tools

GhostLab Spirit Speak hears spoken questions, then answers. The same toolkit also has Spirit Box and Music Box. GhostTube leans on dictionary / environment-to-words tools (for example in Original) and a VOX spirit-box style app. Different input, different product. Neither is proof.
