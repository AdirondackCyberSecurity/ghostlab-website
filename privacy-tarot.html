GhostLab: Divination Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026 · Applies to the iOS and Android app “GhostLab: Divination” and the web app at ghostlab.icu/divination

GhostLab: Divination is an on-device lab for entertainment and reflection: tarot, oracle plates, and an eight ball. It has no user accounts and no servers of our own. Your readings, journal entries, and settings are stored only on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

Summary

  • We never see your readings. Cards, spreads, and journal entries stay on your device.
  • No accounts and no sign-in. The app works entirely offline apart from ads.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. A one-time Pro purchase removes them.

Data the app uses on your device

GhostLab: Divination asks iOS for permission to use the following, strictly to power the features described. This processing happens on your iPhone:

  • Microphone — lets you speak a question or statement before a reading. The audio is used in the moment and is never stored.
  • Speech recognition — turns your spoken question into text. Depending on your device settings, Apple may process speech audio on its servers to provide recognition; see Apple’s Privacy Policy. GhostLab itself never receives or stores this audio.

You can decline or revoke either permission in iOS Settings. The spoken-question feature will simply be unavailable and everything else keeps working.

Advertising

The free version of GhostLab: Divination shows a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad between sittings. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We configure every ad request as non-personalized, and the app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework, the Android advertising ID, or the advertising identifier to track you.

To serve and measure ads, Google receives standard request data from your device: IP address (which can imply an approximate, city-level location), device model and operating system details, and ad interaction data such as which ads were shown and tapped. Apple’s SKAdNetwork may report ad conversions to ad networks in aggregate, without identifying you. Google describes its handling of this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchasing GhostLab Divination Pro removes all advertising from the app.

Purchases

GhostLab Divination Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We receive no payment details. You can restore the purchase on a new device from inside the app. On Android the same one-time unlock is handled by Google Play Billing. Nothing about the purchase reaches us.

The web app at ghostlab.icu/divination

The same three instruments also run as a web app you can add to your Home Screen. It follows the same rules: your pulls, readings, and notes are stored only in your browser (local storage on this device) and never reach us. There is no account. It does not use the microphone.

  • Ads. The free web version shows a banner from Google AdSense. We request non-personalized ads. Google receives the standard request data described above, and in regions that require it Google shows a consent prompt before any ad loads. See the Google advertising policies.
  • Pro on the web. The one-time ad-free unlock is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment details and tax. We never see your card. You receive a license key; entering it in the app sends only that key to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is valid. The key is then kept in your browser. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.
  • Offline. A service worker caches the app and card art on your device so it opens without a connection. Clearing site data removes the cache, your notes, and your key.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not upload your readings, journal, or audio anywhere.

Children

The app carries the age rating shown on its App Store and Google Play pages and is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
