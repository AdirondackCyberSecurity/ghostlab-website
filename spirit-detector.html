Dots

Every dot is a measured distance.

Your iPhone has a laser scanner next to the camera. It measures how far away things are, and Spirit Detector draws a dot for every measurement, many thousands of them every second.

  • Color is distance: close surfaces glow green and yellow, the far wall fades to teal and blue
  • Dots stay where they were measured, so walking the room builds a picture of all of it
  • Freeze stops adding dots so you can walk around what you have
  • Four color looks, plus dot size and glow for sharp detail or a bright sheet on video

On the hunt

How investigators use it.

Spirit Detector shows you the room's surfaces as the scanner sees them. That makes it a mapping tool, a watch on a doorway, and a way to rule out the ordinary before you call anything unexplained.

Map the location first

Sweep each room before the lights go out and record it. You come away with a picture of the doorways, stairs and furniture to plan your setup around and to check against later.

Watch for a shape off the wall

A person-sized shape standing clear of the wall or floor, with nothing behind it, shows up green in front of blue. That is the moment to stop and look closer.

Freeze it and walk around

Tap Freeze and circle what you found. A shape that holds from every angle is a surface the scanner measured. What that surface is, you decide.

Keep scanning in the dark

The scanner measures with its own light, so the dots keep coming when the room is dark. Tracking holds better with some light, and the flashlight is one tap away.

Record for the review

Park the phone on a doorway or hallway and record. Stills and video land in the Library, ready to watch back, share with the team, or save to Photos.

Know what fools the scanner

Glass, mirrors, dark paint and the edges of objects confuse the sensor, and people walking through leave a trail. All of that is normal for LiDAR. Knowing it is half of ruling things out.

Run a sweep

Four steps to a clean map.

The same routine the app teaches on its How it works screen.

  1. 01 Let it fill

    Hold the phone at chest height and let the room fill in for a few seconds before you move.

  2. 02 Walk slowly

    Turn through the room at walking pace or slower. Fast turns smear the map.

  3. 03 Freeze and circle

    Stop adding dots and walk around what you have. Real surfaces hold their shape from every side.

  4. 04 Record, then stand still

    Tap Record and hold steady. Tap the red timer to stop; the video waits in the Library.
