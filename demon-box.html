Demon Box

Uncover the darkness.

Now on iOS · Android coming soon!

GhostLab: Demon Box is a spirit box instrument and a paranormal investigation tool for investigators who are ready to go further into the dark. Start a hunt, ask your question out loud and watch the rune ring. Words and phrases rise inside the ring and are spoken back to you, and what they mean is yours to decide.

Now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Android coming soon. $4.99 once and it's yours for life, with no subscription, no ads and nothing more to buy.

Ask, then listen

Ask your question out loud.

Start a hunt, ask your question and give it a moment. Words and phrases rise inside the ring of runes as a voice speaks them back to you.

  • In Sensor + Voice it hears what you ask, with speech recognition that runs on the phone itself
  • In Sensor mode you set the phone down and let the room speak for itself
  • A jump on the magnetometer, a change in air pressure, a knock on the table or a rap on the wall can each set off a word
See what set it off

Every word shows its reading.

A tag above the ring shows which reading set off each word, with the reading behind it.

  • MAG, AIR and KNOCK tags for the magnetometer, air pressure and knocks
  • A live EMF meter runs along the bottom of the hunt screen. Tap it to zero it where you stand
  • It reads the magnetometer, barometer, accelerometer, gyroscope, compass and location, and hears knocks and raps through the microphone
  • Your own footsteps and handling are kept out of the readings
Nothing is lost

Every hunt is recorded.

Each hunt keeps the sound of the room and every word spoken, so you can go back over it later.

  • Play it back in your Library and read each word with the moment it came
  • The questions you asked sit in the log beside what came back
  • Star a hunt and mark moments as you listen
  • Save a clip of the screen and its sound to share
Make it yours

Choose who speaks.

Pick the voice it speaks with and shape it with voice presets. All four voices work offline.

  • Amy and Michael, The Lost Girl, and Shadow and Whisper, a deep voice with a whisper behind it that is best on headphones
  • Add your own words and names
  • List any words it should never say
  • Words in 16 languages, Latin included
Every hunt has a shape

It changes the longer you stay.

  1. 01 It begins

    The first words are simple words of presence.

  2. 02 It grows restless

    Then it warns you and closes in.

  3. 03 It can be stirred

    Taunts and knocks stir it further. Some spirits lie, and one may pretend to be someone you love.

  4. 04 It fades

    Leave it long enough and it fades with the glow of the ring.

iPhone, iPad and Android

Runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and on Android 8 or later. Asking out loud in Sensor + Voice needs speech recognition on the phone, which on Android means Android 13 or later. No account and no sign-in.

Stays on your phone

No account and no ads. Your hunts and recordings stay on your device until you share them. The app counts how it is used through Google Analytics for Firebase and never sends your recordings or the words in your hunts. Privacy policy

One price

Demon Box is a paid download of $4.99, once. Everything is included, with no subscription and no in-app purchases, and it works offline.
