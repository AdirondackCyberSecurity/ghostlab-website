# GhostLab: Marketing & Brand Strategy

**Prepared for:** Adirondack Cyber Security / GhostLab product leadership  
**Product:** GhostLab — Paranormal Investigation Toolkit (iOS)  
**Horizon:** 12 months (with 90-day launch sprint)  
**Primary client mandate:** Scale awareness via **YouTube personalities in the paranormal / investigation genre**

---

## 1. Executive thesis

GhostLab does not win by claiming “it finds ghosts.” That promise is crowded, legally fragile, and brand-toxic.

GhostLab wins as **the modern investigator’s field kit**: serious-looking tools, honest entertainment framing, on-device privacy, a clean free core, a one-time Pro unlock, and a **signature multi-person feature** (Team offline push-to-talk) that content creators can *film*.

**Positioning line (master):**

> **GhostLab — The investigation toolkit for when the lights go out.**

**Category we own:** *Mobile field investigation entertainment* (not “ghost detector apps,” not horror games).

**Business model narrative:**  
Seven free modes forever. Pro is **$9.99 once** — not a subscription. Creators and fans both respect that.

## 2. Situation snapshot (product truth → marketing truth)

| Product truth | Marketing implication |
|---|---|
| Entertainment / experimental only; not scientific proof | Brand voice = **honest investigator**, never huckster |
| Free: EMF, EVP, Spirit Box (Classic), White Noise, MEVP, Phonetic, DR60 + Camera/Torch/Radar/Knock | Lead with **real toolkit density** free users get tonight |
| Pro: Spirit Speak, SLS, Team + PTT, Creator Mode, banks/FX, Vigil, Estes, Music Box, etc. | Pro is **team + content + advanced instruments**, not “remove ads” |
| On-device, no ads, no accounts, Team is LAN/offline | Trust wedge vs sketchy paranormal apps |
| Team PTT FAB is always reachable mid-hunt | **#1 creator demo** — radio moments on camera |
| Verify instruments | Professionalism cue — “we check the gear” |
| Dark UI, mint + violet spectral system, GhostLab face icon | Strong, ownable visual identity |

**Competitive frame:**  
Most store competitors sell **magic meters**. GhostLab sells **workflow** (mode → tools → tags → review → export) and **crew ops** (Team radio).

## 3. Brand strategy

### 3.1 Brand platform

| Pillar | Meaning | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| **Field-ready** | Built for dark buildings, not couch novelty | Modes, tools grid, torch/camera, verify instruments |
| **Honest** | We name pareidolia, contamination, entertainment limits | Disclaimers, guide copy, no “contact the dead” claims |
| **Private** | Your investigation doesn’t feed an ad network | No accounts / analytics SDKs / on-device storage |
| **Crew-capable** | Solo or multi-person hunts | Team Multipeer + PTT, corroboration, shared tags |
| **Creator-native** | Made for capture and share | Creator Mode, 9:16 export, clips, spectrogram storytelling |

### 3.2 Brand personality

- **Voice:** Calm, precise, slightly dark — like a good field tech lead, not a carnival barker.  
- **Tone spectrum:** 70% professional investigator / 20% cinematic atmosphere / 10% dry wit.  
- **Never:** “Guaranteed spirits,” medical claims, grief exploitation, fake scientific seals.  
- **Always:** “Experimental,” “entertainment,” “document what you captured.”

### 3.3 Visual system (already strong — codify it)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Primary accent | Spectral mint `#3DFFA8` |
| Secondary | Magenta `#C759F2` (Pro / Live Chat) |
| Warning / ON AIR | Orange `#FF8C40` (PTT energy) |
| Base | Near-black navy `#07090F` |
| Hero mark | GhostLab face icon (already iconic) |
| Motion motif | Soft orb glow, radio ring, waveform |

**Brand rule:** Off-store ads and YouTube sponsorships must **match** App Store / site creatives (message match lifts conversion).

### 3.4 Brand architecture

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **GhostLab** | Master brand (consumer) |
| **GhostLab Pro** | One-time unlock product |
| **Team Radio** | Feature brand (creator hook) |
| **Adirondack Cyber Security** | Credibility parent (trust / support / privacy) |

## 4. Audience strategy

### Primary: Content-native investigators (18–34)
- Watch paranormal YouTube / TikTok  
- Go to locations with friends  
- Care about **radio, video, clip-worthy moments**  
- Gate: Team PTT, Creator Mode, SLS, Spirit Speak  

### Secondary: Serious hobbyists (25–45)
- Want “real toolkit” feel, spectrogram, tags, exports  
- Hate subscriptions and ad spam  
- Gate: free modes forever + lifetime Pro  

### Tertiary: Casual spooky-season downloaders
- Halloween spike, one-night fun  
- Gate: free EMF / Spirit Box / atmosphere UI  

### Persona for YouTube targeting
**“Crew Camera”** — films hunts, needs something on-screen that *does something* when the team splits up.  
**“Radio Lead”** — coordinates from base, needs PTT.  
**“Evidence Editor”** — cares about review, tags, spectrogram, PDF/report story.

## 6. YouTube personality strategy (core mandate)

### 6.1 Strategic rationale

Paranormal YouTube is a **proof-of-life medium**. Viewers believe what they see peers use on location. GhostLab’s advantages on camera:

1. **Visible UI** (meters, ghosts stage, mint/violet glow)  
2. **Audible product moment** (PTT “ON AIR,” radio discipline)  
3. **Crew narrative** (split groups, base radio, “check EMF on your end”)  
4. **Honest disclaimers** protect creator reputation (aligned with serious channels)

### 6.2 Creator tiers (who to pursue)

| Tier | Profile | Goal | Deal shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Marquee** | 1M–10M+ subs, location series, high production | Brand legitimacy + spike | Paid integration + product credit + exclusive “Team Radio” segment |
| **B — Workhorses** | 100K–1M, weekly hunts, strong community | Volume + mid-funnel | Paid + affiliate/Pro codes + Custom Product Page |
| **C — Micro crews** | 10K–100K, local teams, raw authenticity | Always-on content | Gifted Pro + free tools + template scripts + leaderboard |
| **D — Emerging Shorts** | High Shorts velocity, not huge subs | Clip supply | Gifted + seed content kit |

**Prioritize B + C first** for ROI and learning; use 1–2 A-tier for brand moments per half-year.

### 6.3 Ideal creator fit score (score 1–5 each)

- Films multi-person investigations  
- Shows phones/tools on camera already  
- Tone not pure gore / not pure comedy-only  
- Willing to say “entertainment / not scientific proof”  
- Audience geo aligns with iOS markets (US, UK, CA, AU first)  
- Open to **unique link** or App Store custom product page  

**Disqualify:** Channels that require “talking to dead relatives,” medical/spiritual healing claims, or refuse honesty language.

### 6.4 Genre map (target lists to build)

Recruit **types**, not only famous names:

1. **Location series hosts** (abandoned hospitals, hotels, prisons)  
2. **Team investigation channels** (3–6 person crews)  
3. **Tech-for-ghost-hunting** reviewers  
4. **EVP / audio-focused** creators  
5. **Female-led & diverse crews** (under-served in sponsorships; strong trust)  
6. **Regional teams** (South, Midwest, UK haunted tourism)  
7. **Crossover:** true crime adjacent “investigation” tones (careful, keep entertainment frame)

*Note: Do not open with cold “pay us to lie about evidence.” Open with “show the toolkit + radio workflow.”*

### 6.5 The GhostLab Creator Playbook (what they film)

Ship a **Creator Kit** (PDF + sample clips + talking points):

| Segment (30–90s) | What to film | Product moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | Dark hallway, radio crackle | PTT “ON AIR” ring |
| **Gear up** | Verify instruments | Professionalism |
| **Split the team** | Two phones, session code | Team Multipeer |
| **Base / mobile** | One person outside, one inside | Hold-to-talk |
| **Evidence pass** | Library / spectrogram / tags | Serious review |
| **Honesty close** | “Entertainment toolkit, not proof” | Brand trust |

**Signature series concept (offer to partners):**  
**“Radio Check”** — recurring segment: every episode, one genuine Team PTT moment + one free-mode demo (EMF or Spirit Box).

### 6.6 Deal structures (clean, scalable)

| Model | When | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| **Gifted Pro + seed** | Micro / early | Free Pro unlock codes, asset pack, tracking link |
| **Flat-fee integration** | Mid tier | 60–90s midroll + description link + pinned comment |
| **Performance hybrid** | Preferred | Smaller fee + $ per first install or Pro unlock (via custom product page / promo code if available) |
| **Whitelisting** | Always negotiate | Rights to run their clip as Spark/ads |
| **Seasonal exclusivity** | A-tier only | Limited category exclusivity (investigation apps) for 30–60 days |

**Tracking stack:**

- Unique App Store **Custom Product Page** per major creator (screenshots: Team PTT first)  
- Unique URL in description  
- Optional vanity: `ghostlab.app/c/NAME` redirect  
- UTM → analytics where possible; App Analytics by CPP  
- Judge success on **install quality + Pro rate**, not views alone  

### 6.7 Outreach sequence (operating system)

1. **Build list of 150** creators (fit score)  
2. **Warm 40** with genuine comments / community presence (2–3 weeks)  
3. **Seed 25** gifted kits (no hard sell)  
4. **Convert 10** to paid/hybrid after they organically post or reply  
5. **Book 2 A-tier** after proof reels and case studies exist  

**Pitch email angle (essence):**

> We’re not asking you to claim GhostLab contacts spirits. We’re giving crews a real toolkit + offline Team radio so the *investigation logistics* are filmable. Free core forever; Pro is one-time. Full honesty language welcome — it matches your audience’s BS detector.

### 6.8 Content guardrails for creators (protect brand + them)

**Required:**

- “Entertainment / experimental investigation”  
- No guarantees of paranormal contact  
- No targeting grieving people as a sales angle  

**Encouraged:**

- Contamination talk (HVAC, trucks, phones)  
- Verify instruments on camera  
- Multi-device Team demo  

**Creative assets we provide:**

- 15s / 30s / 60s vertical cuts of UI  
- App icon + wordmark pack  
- CPP screenshot set: “Team Radio” and “Spirit Box” variants  
- Talk track cards (teleprompter-ready)  

### 6.9 90-day YouTube sprint (mandate execution)

| Week | Actions |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Creator list of 150; kit v1; CPP “Team Radio” live; landing `team` page live |
| 3–4 | Seed 15 micro/mid creators; film internal demo reels for outreach |
| 5–8 | 5–8 public integrations live; measure CPP CVR; iterate talk tracks |
| 9–10 | Case study (“Crew cut CPI / raised Pro”) |
| 11–12 | 1 larger mid-tier or A-tier pilot; plan Halloween content calendar |

**Halloween (annual peak):**  
Lock creators by **end of August**. October inventory disappears early in this genre.

- **Apple Search Ads:** brand + “spirit box app,” “EVP recorder,” competitor conquest carefully

## 9. Community & PR

- Treat local investigation teams as a **street team** (gifted Pro, Team features)  
- Reddit / forums: participate as builders, not spammers  
- PR angle: “Privacy-first investigation toolkit” + “offline team radio for hunts” (tech + culture press)  
- Avoid tabloid “app talks to ghosts” headlines — they convert badly and create review risk

## 10. Path forward (12-month roadmap)

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Weeks 0–4)
- Finalize brand book (1-pager + full)  
- Creator kit v1 + legal honesty language  
- CPPs live (Team, Spirit Box)  
- Marketing site multi-page live  
- Tracking sheet for creators  

### Phase 1 — Proof (Months 1–3)
- Seed program + first paid mid-tier integrations  
- Learn: which talk track drives Pro  
- Ship any creator-requested friction fixes (onboarding to Team)  
- Review velocity campaign (happy path users)  

### Phase 2 — Scale (Months 4–8)
- Always-on micro-creator pipeline  
- Quarterly marquee integration  
- Apple Ads scale on winning keywords  
- Optional: Android only if product parity includes Team story (don’t split brand early if iOS is the wedge)  

### Phase 3 — Peak & systemize (Months 9–12)
- Halloween always-on calendar  
- Evergreen “Radio Check” franchise with 3–5 partner crews  
- Affiliate-style creator portal if volume justifies  
- Brand lift check: unaided “investigation app” mentions in comments

## 11. Budget framework (illustrative, flexible)

For a lean indie / small studio profile:

| Bucket | Share of marketing spend |
|---|---|
| Creator fees + product seeding | 45% |
| Creative production (edits, kits, CPP art) | 15% |
| Apple Ads | 20% |
| Tools (ASO, analytics, link tracking) | 10% |
| Contingency / tests | 10% |

**Rule:** Never spend more on a creator than you can measure against **30-day Pro revenue + retained free users** from that source after 2 cycles of learning.

## 12. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Creator implies scientific proof | Contractual honesty clause + provided script lines |
| Low-quality install spikes | CPP + post-install retention review; cut partners who fail quality |
| App Review / positioning issues | Keep entertainment framing everywhere |
| Brand looks “gimmick app” | Lead with Team workflow + review tools, not floating orbs only |
| Halloween-only seasonality | Year-round Radio Check + spring abandoned-location calendar |

## 14. Closing note from the “agency chair”

GhostLab’s unfair advantage is not another EMF needle. It is **credible craft + crew radio + privacy + a fair free tier**, in a category full of junk.

YouTube personalities will promote what:

1. Looks good on camera,  
2. Creates a moment in the story,  
3. Doesn’t make them look like grifters.

Team push-to-talk + honest toolkit positioning is that product. The path forward is to **industrialize creator partnerships** the way a CPG brand industrializes retail demos — kits, scripts, exclusives, measurement — while protecting the brand’s integrity like a regulated category.

**Win condition (12 months):**  
GhostLab is the default phone app serious amateur crews and mid-tier YouTube teams mention when they say *“phones out, radio check.”*
