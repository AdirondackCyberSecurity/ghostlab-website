GhostLab: Eight Ball Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026 · Applies to the iOS and Android app “GhostLab: Eight Ball”

GhostLab: Eight Ball is a toy. You ask a yes or no question, shake the phone or tap the ball, and a triangular die settles on one of twenty printed faces. Everything happens on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of our own.

Summary

  • Nothing you ask leaves your phone. The question is never sent anywhere, and the app keeps no history of it.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. A one-time Pro purchase removes them.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Your question — held in memory while you ask it, and gone when you leave the screen. It is never written to a file and never uploaded.
  • Your settings — the finish in the chamber, the sound switch, and the fact that you have read the disclaimer are stored in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Motion — the shake is read from the device's motion sensors while the chamber is on screen, to know when to turn the die. Nothing about that motion is recorded or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free version of GhostLab: Eight Ball shows a banner under the chamber and an occasional full-screen ad after an answer settles. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We configure every ad request as non-personalized. On iOS the app does not use App Tracking Transparency or the advertising identifier to track you; on Android it does not ask for the advertising ID for tracking.

To serve and measure ads, Google receives standard request data from your device: IP address (which can imply an approximate, city-level location), device model and operating system details, and ad interaction data such as which ads were shown and tapped. Your question is never part of any ad request. Google describes its handling of this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchasing Eight Ball Pro removes all advertising from the app.

Purchases

Eight Ball Pro is a one-time purchase, handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android. We receive no payment details. The GhostLab Sphere Pass, also a one-time purchase, unlocks Pro across the GhostLab apps: on iOS the entitlement is recorded in your device keychain and in Apple's iCloud key-value store on your own Apple ID, and on Android each GhostLab app asks its siblings on the same device, which only apps signed with the same certificate can answer. We never see it. You can restore either purchase on a new device from inside the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not use a microphone or a camera.
  • We do not upload your questions or your answers anywhere.

Children

The app carries the age rating shown on its App Store and Google Play pages and is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
