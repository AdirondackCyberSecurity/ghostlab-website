Sharing map tags

  • A tag records the spot you chose on the Haunts map, along with the name, note and icon you gave it. Your phone reads your location only while you are making a tag, and you can drag the pin anywhere before saving.
  • A shared tag travels inside the link or over your Teams session. GhostLab doesn’t store tags on its servers, and the ghostlab.icu page that shows a tag reads it from the link in your browser.
  • Anyone who has a tag link can open it. If a friend forwards it, the next person can see the spot too, so share links only with people you trust.
  • Shared tags leave other people’s maps when they expire: after 7 days, or when you delete the tag if you chose to keep it until then. A link already sent, or a screenshot, can’t be recalled.
  • You’re responsible for every tag you share, including its name and note.
  • You can report or block anyone who sends you a tag. Blocking hides their tags on your device, and they are not told.
  • Never tag your home or anyone else’s home, and never use tags to follow, watch or find a person who has not agreed to it.
