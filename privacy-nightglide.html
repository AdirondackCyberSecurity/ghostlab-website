Night Glide Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026 · Applies to the iOS and Android app “Night Glide”

Night Glide is a spirit-board experience made for entertainment. It has no user accounts and no servers of our own. The questions you ask and the answers the board gives stay on your device.

Summary

  • Séances stay on your phone. Session history and board memory are stored only on your device.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. A one-time Plus purchase removes them.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Local network — lets two phones in the same room share one séance. The connection is peer to peer between the two devices; nothing passes through our servers. You can decline this permission in iOS Settings and single-phone séances keep working.
  • Board memory — the app remembers parts of earlier séances (such as names it has heard) so later sessions can feel continuous. This memory lives only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The free version of Night Glide shows a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad when a séance ends. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We configure every ad request as non-personalized, and the app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework, the Android advertising ID, or the advertising identifier to track you.

To serve and measure ads, Google receives standard request data from your device: IP address (which can imply an approximate, city-level location), device model and operating system details, and ad interaction data such as which ads were shown and tapped. Apple’s SKAdNetwork may report ad conversions to ad networks in aggregate, without identifying you. Google describes its handling of this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchasing Night Glide Plus removes all advertising from the app.

Purchases

Night Glide Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We receive no payment details. You can restore the purchase on a new device from inside the app. On Android the same one-time unlock is handled by Google Play Billing, and the Sphere Pass reaches the other GhostLab apps by asking the copies installed on the same device, which only apps signed with the same certificate can answer. Nothing about either purchase reaches us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not upload your questions, séances, or board memory anywhere.

Children

Night Glide carries the age rating shown on its App Store and Google Play pages and is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
