GhostLab Community Guidelines

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · For map tags you share and evidence you submit to the gallery

The gallery is a place to show the strangest thing your hunt turned up, and tags are how your team finds the cold spot again at 2 am. These rules keep both of them safe for the people in them, the places they happen in, and the people looking. Everything submitted to the gallery is read or watched by a person before it goes up.

Share what you actually captured

  • Post your own recordings and photos, from your own investigation.
  • Keep the moment that matters unedited. Trimming, brightening and adding GhostLab’s own overlays are fine. Added effects, staged scenes and clips from someone else’s video are not allowed.
  • Say what you think it is, and also what else it could be. Posts that show you tried to explain it away get featured more often.

Respect places and the people who own them

  • Only post from places you had permission to be. Hunts at night in closed cemeteries, condemned buildings or posted private land don’t belong here.
  • Name the place as the venue or town. Don’t post street addresses, gate codes, ways to get in, or anything that would help someone trespass.
  • Leave graves, remains and memorials as you found them. Don’t post photos of human remains.
  • Don’t accuse a real, identifiable person or business of a crime, abuse or a cover-up in a title or caption.

Protect the people in your shot

  • Anyone recognisable must have agreed to be shown. Blur faces you don’t have permission for, or we will.
  • No children who can be recognised, in any submission, ever.
  • Hide house numbers, licence plates and anything else that pins down where someone lives.
  • Never use a tag to track, watch or find a person who hasn’t agreed to it, and never tag a home.

Content we remove

  • Sexual content or nudity of any kind, and anything that sexualises a minor. We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
  • Gore, real injuries, animal cruelty, self-harm, and real crime scenes.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, and bullying, including about anyone’s religion or beliefs.
  • Music, film or TV audio, or anyone else’s work you don’t have the rights to.
  • Offers to sell or buy paranormal services, readings, cleansings, haunted objects or tours, and any other advertising or spam.
  • Instructions for dangerous stunts, rituals meant to scare or hurt someone, or anything illegal.

Reporting and blocking

Every tag and gallery item has a Report option, and every tag has Block. Reports go to a person, usually within 48 hours. If something puts someone in danger right now, contact local emergency services first. Owners of a place and people shown in a submission can ask for it to come down at ghostlabsupport@inkboxmail.com without needing a legal reason.

What happens when rules are broken

Most problems end with the item coming down and a note explaining why. Repeated or serious problems pause your ability to share and submit, first for days and then for good. Anything involving a child’s safety or a real threat is removed immediately and reported.
