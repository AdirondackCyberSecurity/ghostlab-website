GhostLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: EFFECTIVE_DATE_AT_MERGE · Applies to “GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit” on iOS and Android (formerly SpectralEVP)

GhostLab is built to be private by design. There are no user accounts, no ads in the app, and no servers of our own. Your investigation recordings, videos, notes, and settings are stored only on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Two things leave your phone on their own: a purchase-attribution signal that credits the creator who referred a sale, described under Affiliate attribution, and usage statistics that show us which tools people use, described under Usage statistics.

Summary

  • Your recordings stay with you. Nothing you record in GhostLab is transmitted to us.
  • No accounts and no ads in the app. We never build an advertising profile of you and we never sell your information. Our Google Ads campaigns use the Android usage statistics to measure how they perform, as described under Usage statistics.
  • Usage statistics. The app counts how its tools are used through Google Analytics for Firebase, on iPhone, iPad, and Android. It never sends your recordings, anything you type or say, your name, or your GPS location. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland it sends none at all.
  • Everything stays on your device unless you export, share, or save it somewhere yourself.
  • Tags you send carry a location. A shared tag link holds the spot’s exact location and your name. It goes only to the people you send it to, and never to us.
  • Purchases credit a creator. When you buy GhostLab Pro, an app-scoped device identifier and the details of that purchase go to our affiliate partner so the right creator gets paid.

Data the app uses on your device

GhostLab asks your phone for permission to use the following, strictly to power the features described. All of this processing happens on your own device:

  • Microphone - records EVP investigation sessions and powers Spirit Speak Live Chat. Recordings are saved to the app’s on-device Library.
  • Speech recognition - Spirit Speak uses Apple’s speech recognition to loosely match spoken questions to dictionary words. Depending on your device settings, Apple may process speech audio on its servers to provide recognition; see Apple’s Privacy Policy. GhostLab itself never receives or stores this audio off-device.
  • Names you say aloud - names you say aloud during a Spirit Speak hunt (for example, “my name is Jack”) may be remembered on this device, with the time they were last heard, so the spirit can recognize you in a later hunt. They never leave the device, and deleting the app erases them.
  • Camera - provides the low-light overlay, Motion-Grid, SLS body-tracking view, and optional session video recording. Video is saved with the investigation on this device.
  • Photos (add only) - only when you tap “Save to Photos,” the app writes a video to your Photos library. GhostLab cannot read your existing photos.
  • Location (while using the app) - shows haunted places near you, logs compass heading, tags where a session was recorded so it can appear on your in-app map, and places the spots you tag on the Haunts map. Location stays in the session files and tags on your device until you choose to share a tag, as described under Shared tags. During a Spirit Speak hunt, the app may send your current position to Apple's address lookup (on iPhone) or Google's (on Android) to learn what kind of place you are near, such as a cemetery or a church, so the spirit's words can fit it. Only that place type is used, in memory for that hunt, and it is never saved with the session.
  • Motion & Fitness (barometer) - reads barometric pressure during sessions to log environmental pressure trends alongside your recordings.
  • Local network (Team mode) - offline Multipeer connectivity shares session tags and live levels directly between nearby investigators’ phones. This is peer-to-peer on your local network; nothing passes through the internet or any server.
  • Nearby Interaction (ultra-wideband) - measures distance to a second U1-capable device used as a trigger object during investigations.
  • Apple Watch companion - start/stop/tag commands sync between your Watch and iPhone via Apple’s WatchConnectivity, device-to-device.

You can decline or revoke any permission in your device settings; the related feature simply won’t function.

Shared tags

On the Haunts map you can tag a spot and send it to a friend. Sharing makes a link that holds the spot’s exact location, what you found there, the name and note you gave it, your name, and when you made it. The link is signed with a key kept on your phone, so the receiver’s app can tell it hasn’t been changed. GhostLab has no server for tags. The link travels only in the message you send, and we never receive it. Anyone you send it to, and anyone they forward it to, can see what it holds.

Opening a link shows a page on ghostlab.icu. The tag sits in the part of the link after the “#”, which your browser reads on your phone and never sends to our website. The page loads no map on its own. If you tap “Show the map here,” the map comes from OpenStreetMap, which then receives the area being shown; see the OpenStreetMap privacy policy. The Apple Maps and Google Maps buttons hand the location to those apps.

A tag a friend sends you is saved on your device. It leaves your map after 7 days unless the sender chose to let you keep it, and you can remove any tag yourself. If you report a tag, your email app opens a message to GhostLab support that contains the tag link. We use it only to look into the report and delete it once that is done.

Purchases

The optional GhostLab Pro lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We never see or store your name, payment details, or billing information. Your entitlement is verified on-device through Apple’s StoreKit or Google Play Billing.

Affiliate attribution

Creators who recommend GhostLab earn a share of the sales they bring in. To work out which creator referred a purchase, GhostLab includes the GoMarketMe affiliate SDK. On first launch and again when a Pro purchase completes, it sends the following to GoMarketMe:

  • An app-scoped device identifier. On iOS this is Apple’s identifier for vendor, which is unique to GhostLab on your device and resets when you delete the app. Android uses an equivalent installation identifier.
  • Basic device information: model, operating system version, screen size, language, and country.
  • Details of the purchase itself: the product, price, currency, transaction identifier, and time.

GhostLab does not use the Apple advertising identifier or the Android advertising ID, and it never asks for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. This information is used to credit an affiliate and to report sales totals. It is not used to show you advertising, it is not combined with data from other apps, and it is never sold. GoMarketMe processes it on our behalf under their privacy policy.

Usage statistics

GhostLab: Paranormal Toolkit uses Google Analytics for Firebase on iPhone, iPad, and Android to count how the app is used, so we can see which tools people use and where they get stuck. It records:

  • What you do in the app, in general terms: finishing the first screens, starting and saving a session and which tool it used (for example EVP or Spirit Box), how long a saved session ran, sharing a file or a tag (the kind of item, such as a clip or a report, and the app you shared to when it is a well-known one such as TikTok or YouTube), opening the GhostLab Pro screen, starting the free trial or using a free try, and how a Pro purchase ended, with its price and currency.
  • An app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for GhostLab on your device. It is not linked to your name, your Apple or Google account, or your device’s advertising identifier, and it resets if you delete the app (or, on Android, clear its data). It is separate from the identifier our affiliate partner receives, and the two are never combined.
  • Basic device and app details: device model, iOS, iPadOS, or Android version, app version, language, and country.
  • Your approximate area, such as your city and country, which Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address.

It never records your recordings, video, photos, or transcripts, anything you type or say, session names and notes, the names you say aloud to Spirit Speak, what your tags contain, or your GPS location. GhostLab does not collect the advertising identifier on any device. On Android the permission is removed from the app and collection is switched off. The iPhone and iPad app does not include Apple’s advertising framework and never asks to track you.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app sends no usage statistics. They start switched off every time the app opens and turn on only when nothing on the device points to one of those countries: on iPhone and iPad its App Store country and its language and region settings, and on Android its Google Play country, its SIM card, its mobile network, and its language settings.

Google processes this information for us as a service provider. We have turned off Google’s options to use it for its own products, and the app tells Google that it may not be used for personalized advertising.

We link the Android usage statistics to our own Google Ads account, so we can see how many people who found GhostLab through one of our ads went on to use a tool, start the free trial, or buy GhostLab Pro. Google Ads uses those results to measure our campaigns and to decide where to show our ads. We do not send Google Ads any audiences or lists of users, and the statistics from iPhone and iPad are not used for advertising at all.

The recorded actions are kept for 2 months, and the identifier until 2 months after you last use the app. Then Google deletes them automatically.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone. Two services receive information from the app: GoMarketMe receives the affiliate attribution described above so creators can be paid, and Google receives the usage statistics described above, processes them for us, and uses the Android ones to measure our Google Ads campaigns. Apple and Google handle your payment and tell us only whether you own Pro.

Everything else leaves your device only through actions you take yourself: exporting a session package, generating and sharing a PDF report, saving a video to Photos, sending a tag link to a friend, reporting a tag to us, or sharing session tags with nearby teammates in Team mode.

Data retention and deletion

All sessions, recordings, and settings live in the app’s storage on your device. Delete any session from the Library, or delete the app to remove everything at once. We hold no copies of any of it.

Tags you make and tags friends send you are kept on your device the same way. Deleting one of your tags removes it from your phone. Copies already sent to friends stay on their phones until they expire or the friend removes them, because a link that has been sent can’t be called back.

The affiliate attribution records held by GoMarketMe are kept while they are needed to calculate creator payouts and to meet tax and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete them at any time. See Data deletion requests for how to do that.

The recorded usage statistics are deleted automatically after 2 months, and the identifier 2 months after you last use the app. Deleting the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) removes the identifier from your device.

Children

GhostLab is rated 9+ for infrequent horror/fear themes and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how data is handled, we will update this page and note it in the App Store and Google Play release notes before the change takes effect.
