GhostLab: Palmistry Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “GhostLab: Palmistry”

GhostLab: Palmistry reads the creases of your palm from a photo, entirely on your phone, for entertainment. It has no user accounts and no servers of our own. Your palm photos and readings stay on your device.

Summary

  • Palm photos never leave your phone. The photo is analyzed on the device and stored only in the app’s own library.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. A one-time Pro purchase removes them.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Camera — takes the photo of your palm for a sitting. The photo is processed by Apple’s on-device Vision framework and saved only inside the app’s storage on your phone. It is never uploaded. Deleting a sitting, or the app, deletes the photos. You can decline the camera permission in iOS Settings; the app will explain that a sitting needs a photo to work.
  • Readings — the traced lines and their readings are stored with the sitting on your device and shared only if you share them yourself.

Advertising

The free version of GhostLab: Palmistry shows a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad after a sitting. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We configure every ad request as non-personalized, and the app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework or the advertising identifier to track you.

To serve and measure ads, Google receives standard request data from your device: IP address (which can imply an approximate, city-level location), device model and operating system details, and ad interaction data such as which ads were shown and tapped. Your palm photos are never part of any ad request. Apple’s SKAdNetwork may report ad conversions to ad networks in aggregate, without identifying you. Google describes its handling of this data in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchasing GhostLab Palmistry Pro removes all advertising from the app.

Purchases

GhostLab Palmistry Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We receive no payment details. You can restore the purchase on a new device from inside the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run analytics SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not upload your palm photos or readings anywhere.

Children

The app carries the age rating shown on its App Store page and is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
